## 2. What Data We Collect

**a) Behavioral Data**

We collect anonymized, aggregated data about how users interact with our website, such as pages visited, time spent on each page, and clicked links or navigation paths. This data is strictly used to understand website performance and improve user experience.

**b) Information You Provide**

If you contact us via forms, we may collect your name, email address, and message content.

**c) No Retargeting or Personal Identification**

We do not collect data to track individuals, perform retargeting, or associate behavioral data with specific users.

**d) Technically Necessary Cookies**

We use only technically necessary cookies. No consent is required for these under applicable law.

| Cookie | Purpose | Provider | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|----------|
| `cf_clearance` | Protects the website against automated attacks (DDoS, bots) | Cloudflare | max. 30 min |
| `cookie_notice` | Remembers that you acknowledged the cookie notice | Makaio | 1 year |

We do not use any tracking, marketing, or analytics cookies.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data

- To analyze user interactions and improve website functionality.
- To respond to inquiries submitted through contact forms.
- To measure website performance (in an aggregated and anonymized way).

We do not use your data for marketing without explicit consent and do not engage in targeted advertising.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

At Makaio, we do not share, sell, or trade your data with any third parties under any circumstances.

---

## 5. Your Data Rights

If you have submitted personal data via forms, you can:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your submitted data.
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your personal data.

---

## 6. Data Security

We take appropriate measures to secure the data we collect. While we work hard to protect your data, no system is completely secure.

---

## 7. Retention of Data

We retain behavioral data in anonymized, aggregated form for website analysis. Personal data submitted via forms is retained only as long as necessary to address your inquiry or comply with legal obligations.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for individuals under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

---

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, and the "Effective Date" will reflect any updates.
